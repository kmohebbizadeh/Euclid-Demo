Berkeley Hills wildfire mitigation and fuel reduction

Berkeley programs support defensible-space work, vegetation removal, chipping, home hardening, and eucalyptus understory cleanup. East Bay Regional Park District’s 2026 Tilden projects add eucalyptus, brush, and woody-debris removal near the wildland–urban edge.

The model treats these measures as reducing expected loss.

City wildfire programs ↗ Tilden fuels projects ↗

Unusually dry and high-wind summer increases wildfire risk

Hot, critically dry conditions and recurring breezy periods have elevated fire-weather risk across the interior East Bay and its higher terrain. The model also includes an El Niño-linked warm-ocean scenario as an added temperature and fuel-moisture stress signal.

The weather adjustment partially offsets the benefit from mitigation work.
